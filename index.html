About Me

I'm a web developer and software engineer based in Pennsylvania. I build websites and mobile apps that help real businesses connect with their communities.

With a Bachelor's in Web & Interactive Media from Pennsylvania College of Technology and a Master's in Software Engineering from Penn State, I bring solid engineering to every project—custom websites and cross-platform mobile apps alike.

From comic shops to reptile zoos, I've helped some of Pennsylvania's best-known businesses grow online, and I ship mobile apps for iOS and Android. See the websites and the apps.

When I'm not coding, you might find me fishing, hiking, or playing video games.
